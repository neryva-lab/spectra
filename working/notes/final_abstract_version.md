Title

BPGS: Bounded Precision-Geometry Scaling for Robust Multi-Task Learning under Extreme Loss-Scale Heterogeneity

Abstract

Multi-task learning often combines objectives with substantially different numerical scales, which can destabilize adaptive task weighting and produce pathological uncertainty estimates. We introduce Bounded Precision-Geometry Scaling (BPGS), a weighting method that constrains learned uncertainty parameters through a sigmoid reparameterization, preventing the unbounded drift that can arise in homoscedastic uncertainty weighting under severe scale mismatch. BPGS includes a batch-aware variant that normalizes task precisions using running statistics of per-task log-losses and an optional auto-calibration step from the first batch. We evaluate BPGS on synthetic and real multi-task benchmarks. On a pure post-hoc loss-rescaling stress test, BPGS maintains a macro-score of about 0.77-0.78 from x1 to x1000 scaling, while Kendall-style weighting drops from 0.77 to 0.63 at x1000. On NYUv2, the canonical batch-aware configuration improves surface-normal error over Kendall (35.18 vs. 38.68 degrees) and slightly improves depth absolute relative error (0.287 vs. 0.294), while the batch-aware fixed variant achieves the lowest overall loss (2.488) and best depth metrics among BPGS ablations. On Yeast and RF1, BPGS remains competitive with static weighting, PCGrad, and Kendall, achieving 0.640 micro-F1 and 60.69 total loss, respectively, while avoiding the instability observed for UWSO. These results position BPGS as a simple and robust alternative for multi-task optimization under heterogeneous loss scales.
